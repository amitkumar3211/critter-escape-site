Critter Escape

Free the critters! Tap a critter with a clear lane and it runs off the board. Blocked critters slide until they hit something and stay there, so every move counts. 100 hand-tuned levels, an endless mode, a daily challenge with streaks, and a Sanctuary to decorate.
