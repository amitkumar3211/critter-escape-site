Privacy Policy

Critter Escape for Android · Effective 11 October 2026

HomeTerms of Service

This policy explains what information the Critter Escape app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is published by Acumen Bay. If anything here is unclear, write to support@critterescape.app.

Summary

  • You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress, coins, settings and purchases are stored on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob and sells in-app items through Google Play Billing. Those Google services process some device data as described below.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves the following locally, in private app storage that other apps cannot read: current level, stars, coins, boosters, owned and placed Sanctuary items, daily-challenge completion and streaks, settings such as sound, language and appearance, and whether the tutorial has been shown. This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share something (see "Sharing"). Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Information processed by third-party services

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded video ads that you choose to watch for bonuses such as extra moves or double coins. Ads are served by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address, coarse location derived from it, device type and operating system, app identifier, and interaction with the ads. How Google uses this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can reset or delete your advertising identifier and opt out of ad personalization in your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Coin packs, the starter pack and Remove Ads are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the app only that a purchase succeeded, with an order token. We never see your card details. Purchases are governed by Google Play's terms.

Google Play services

The app relies on Google Play services on your device for ads and billing. Google may collect diagnostic and device data under its own policy.

Notifications

With your permission, the app can send one local daily reminder about the daily challenge. Reminders are scheduled on your device; no server is involved. You can turn them off in the app's Settings or in your device's notification settings at any time.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates an image or text of your result and hands it to the app you pick (for example a messaging app). Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose a destination, and the receiving app's policy then applies.

Children

The app is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the app are requested from Google AdMob in a way that follows Google's family and child-directed policies for the audience the app declares on Google Play. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Data held by Google for ads and purchases is retained under Google's policies; you can manage ad data through your Google account and device settings.

Security

Game data is kept in the app's private storage. Payments and ad requests use encrypted connections to Google.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, most requests concern Google's services, and the links above explain how to exercise them with Google. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the app collects, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
